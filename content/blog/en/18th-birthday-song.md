- **Grandparents:** a gift that is more modern than the usual envelope of cash and still comes from the heart.

## Ideas for the lyrics

- **Childhood:** the first day of school, a favorite cuddly toy, family vacations, nicknames.
- **Teenage years:** hobbies, sports club, best friend, legendary parties.
- **Typical of the birthday person:** sayings, quirks and passions like gaming, soccer, music or fashion.
- **Looking ahead:** driver's license, moving out, apprenticeship, university, a trip around the world. And good wishes for everything to come.

Bullet points are enough. You don't have to rhyme anything.

## Which music style suits an 18-year-old?

The song should match the taste of the birthday person, not the taste of the people giving it:

- **Modern Pop / Radio Hit:** modern and catchy. You rarely go wrong with it.
- **EDM / Dance & Club Groove:** for the party, for celebrating and dancing.
- **R&B / Soul / Urban:** groovy and laid-back.
- **Rock & Power Ballad:** for everyone who likes it loud.
- **Heartfelt Pop Ballad:** when parents or grandparents want to say something moving.
