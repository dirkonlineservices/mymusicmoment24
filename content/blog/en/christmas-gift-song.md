## Gift ideas: Who is a Christmas song right for?

### For Mom and Dad
A thank-you song for everything your parents have done for you: the vacations, the advice, the favorite dishes. An emotional ballad or a warm acoustic sound fits well.

### For Grandma and Grandpa
A song about family stories, Sundays together and the grandchildren. Especially for grandparents who don't wish for anything anymore, a song with the names of the whole family is a touching gift.

### For your partner
How did you meet? What was your best moment this year? That becomes a declaration of love for the holidays, romantic as a pop ballad or as a duet with two voices.

### For the whole family
Why just one song? One of my customers ordered a song of their own for every family member. Around 20 songs came out of it, each with its own memories. At Christmas especially, when everyone gets together, that makes for a special moment: everyone gets their own song.

### For friends, your team or your club
People usually talk about a Christmas party with its own song for a long time. For colleagues, the team or your group of friends, a funny sing-along chorus with references to the year you shared works well.
