# Retirement Song: The Farewell Song for Colleagues

After 20, 30 or 40 years with the company, a colleague is retiring. The team looks for a farewell gift and often ends up with a gift basket, a voucher or a card that everyone signs. A **retirement song** is more personal. It tells the story of those years, with all the inside jokes only the team knows.

You'll get it **usually within 24 hours on business days, for a fixed price of €19.99**. For a team of ten people, that's two euros per person.

## Why a song makes a lovely retirement gift

- **It honors the work of an entire career.** That says more than "Thanks and all the best" on a card.
- **It gives the farewell party a special moment:** when the song plays and the team sings along, there's laughter and often tears too.
- **It lasts:** the new retiree can listen to the song again and again at home and think of their colleagues.

## Ideas for the lyrics

Gather as a team what makes this person who they are. Bullet points are enough:

- **The basics:** name, nickname, department, year they started, how many years with the company.
- **Typical sayings:** "We've always done it this way", "Coffee first".
- **Legendary stories:** the Christmas party, the company outing, the project that nearly went wrong.
- **What the team will miss:** good spirits on Mondays, cake on birthdays, help with every problem.
- **What comes next:** garden, grandchildren, travel, motorhome, golf course. And finally sleeping in.

## Example: what your details might look like

> *"Klaus, 'Klausi', in accounting since 1987. Lived through the switch to the euro and survived every computer change. Brings pretzels every Friday. His saying: 'Numbers don't lie.' Looking forward to motorhome trips with his wife Gabi. The team says thank you for 38 years."*

This turns into a song with his saying in the chorus, the pretzels in one verse and the motorhome at the end.
