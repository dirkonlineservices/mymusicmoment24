## Why a song makes such a lovely anniversary gift

Finding a gift for a 25th or 50th wedding anniversary is hard. Most couples celebrating an anniversary have long had everything they need. What you can't buy is **recognition of their shared story**:

- **Their story in music:** How did it all begin? The first meeting, the proposal, the first home together.
- **Times they got through together:** raising children, building a house, getting through hard times and sticking together.
- **The family's thanks:** children and grandchildren say thank you in the chorus for love, advice and support.

When the song plays in the banquet hall, it often goes completely quiet. And then come the happy tears.

## Wedding anniversaries at a glance

In Germany, every wedding anniversary has its own name. Many families use the round and "big" ones for a special gift:

| Years married | German name | Song idea |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 year | Papierne Hochzeit (paper wedding) | A love-struck pop song about the first year together |
| 5 years | Hölzerne Hochzeit (wooden wedding) | An acoustic ballad about the home you share |
| 10 years | Rosenhochzeit (rose wedding) | A romantic duet as a declaration of love |
| 12½ years | Petersilienhochzeit (parsley wedding) | A funny song with inside jokes, halfway to the silver wedding |
| 25 years | **Silberhochzeit (silver wedding)** | A look back at a quarter century, often given by the children |
| 30 years | Perlenhochzeit (pearl wedding) | A heartfelt ballad about loyalty and sticking together |
| 40 years | Rubinhochzeit (ruby wedding) | A warm acoustic song about four decades |
| 50 years | **Goldene Hochzeit (golden wedding)** | The big song of a lifetime, with children, grandchildren and milestones |
| 60 years | Diamantene Hochzeit (diamond wedding) | A dignified ballad or a Schlager to sing along to |
| 65 years | Eiserne Hochzeit (iron wedding) | A thank you from the whole family |

## Silver wedding: 25 years in one song

A quarter century of love is a lovely reason for a musical surprise. Often the grown-up children give the song, or the couple use it as a declaration of love to each other. Pop, a heartfelt ballad or a duet with two voices are popular choices.

## A song for a 50th wedding anniversary: the golden wedding

Half a century full of life, loyalty and family. A **song for a 50th wedding anniversary** tells the whole story: from meeting in the 70s through children, grandchildren and vacations to today's celebration. Schlager, acoustic or a pop ballad with warm voices are often chosen. It's especially moving when the children and grandchildren provide the memories and the chorus becomes their shared thank you.

Companies and clubs also have a **commissioned song created for their anniversary**. For example as a company anthem for the anniversary party, as a club song for the festival weekend or as a thank you to long-standing members and employees.

## Planning music for the anniversary party: how the song becomes the highlight

- **Choose the right moment:** after dinner or right after the speech, when all the guests are there and it's quiet.
- **Announce it, but don't give it away:** "We have one more little surprise" is enough. The couple will realize what it's about when the chorus comes.
- **Provide good speakers:** the MP3 plays on any sound system or Bluetooth speaker. Ideally test it once beforehand.
- **Something to hold:** with the song certificate (see below), the couple also have the lyrics on paper.
- **Listen again later:** with the [streaming release](/en/streaming) (one-time €4.99), family and guests can listen to the song on Spotify afterwards.
