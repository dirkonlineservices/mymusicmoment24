## Which moments is a custom wedding song right for?

A personal song fits many points of the wedding day:

### 1. First dance
This is the classic use and usually the most emotional one. Instead of dancing to Ed Sheeran's "Perfect" like so many couples, you open the evening with a song that is **only about you**. With your names, your story and your declaration of love. Your guests will remember it for a long time.

### 2. A surprise from the wedding party or family
The best man and maid of honor secretly have a personal song written for the couple and play it during the celebration. It usually doesn't take long before the first tissues come out.

### 3. Walking down the aisle or the champagne reception
A quiet acoustic song accompanies the processional or the reception after the ceremony. Soft, personal and made just for you.

### 4. As a lasting wedding gift
The song stays. You play it in the car, save it on Spotify or share it on Instagram. And when you hear it again years later, the whole day comes right back.

### 5. For the cake cutting or an evening act
Suddenly a song with your names is playing in the background. First there's silence, then laughter, then tears. That's exactly how it should be.

## Tips for a wedding song that works

From the songs I've made so far, I know what matters:

**1. Be specific, not general.**  
"He always throws the best barbecue in summer" is better than "He loves food". Specific details create pictures in your head.

**2. Use both names, and pet names too.**  
A wedding song with your real names in the chorus moves people in a completely different way than a generic "you and me".

**3. Choose the genre for the couple, not for yourself.**  
If she secretly loves Schlager, go with Schlager. If he's into acoustic music, go acoustic. The song should move the two of them, not please you.

**4. Think about the mood of the moment.**  
For the first dance, something slow that's easy to dance to works well. A surprise song during the speeches can be livelier and shorter.

**5. Let the wedding party commission the song.**  
That way the couple really knows nothing about it, and the surprise is genuine.
