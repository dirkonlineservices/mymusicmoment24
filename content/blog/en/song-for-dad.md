## What belongs in a song for Dad

The best songs live on small, real details. These questions will help you collect them:

- **What do you call him?** Dad, Pop, Daddy or a nickname of your own?
- **What did you do together?** Soccer on weekends, fishing, tinkering in the garage, long drives to your vacation spot.
- **What is typical of him?** His sayings, his favorite team, his barbecue, his workshop, his music.
- **What are you grateful for?** A moment when he was there when it mattered: when you moved, when your first car broke down, when you had a hard decision to make.
- **Who else is part of it?** Mom, siblings, grandchildren: everyone who should appear in the song.

Bullet points are enough. You don't have to rhyme anything.

## Example: what your details might look like

> *"Dad's name is Thomas, he's turning 65 and retiring. We call him 'Paps'. VfB Stuttgart fan, barbecues in any weather, drove us to Italy in the camper trailer every summer. His saying: 'We'll manage.' Carried every wardrobe himself when we moved. Two grandchildren: Lena and Max. We want to say thank you."*

This turns into a song with his saying in the chorus, the Italian summers in the first verse and the grandchildren at the end.
