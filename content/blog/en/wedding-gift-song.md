## 5 good moments for the wedding song at the celebration

As the best man, maid of honor, a parent or a best friend, you can easily work the song into the wedding day:

### 1. The musical surprise speech
Instead of a long speech, the best man or maid of honor announces a little surprise. Then a song plays that includes the funniest stories from the time the couple got to know each other.

### 2. A soundtrack for the wedding video
Almost every couple has photos and videos taken these days. Their own song as the music for the wedding video makes it even more personal.

### 3. The emotional surprise dance
The groom or the bride surprises their partner with a love song ordered in secret. There are rarely any dry eyes.

### 4. Cutting the wedding cake together
A song in the background turns cutting the cake or the midnight buffet into a special moment.

### 5. The gift you can hold: the song certificate
With the song certificate (+ €9.99), you also have something to hand over. It's a PDF with the lyrics and a **QR code**. Printed and framed, it becomes a keepsake for the living room. With the QR code, the couple can play the song on their smartphone at any time, for example on every wedding anniversary.

## What goes into the lyrics of a custom wedding song?

For the song to really sound like the two of them, concrete details help:
* **How did it all begin?** Where did they meet: at university, at a party or online?
* **The big hurdles and milestones:** Did they make a long-distance relationship work? Their first home together? A move?
* **The proposal:** Romantic on the beach, a surprise on a hike or very quietly at home?
* **Typical quirks:** Who needs three coffees in the morning? Who always forgets the keys?
* **Good wishes for the future:** sticking together, good health and lots of happiness.

From these details come verses and a chorus in the music style you choose, for example as a pop ballad, an acoustic song or a duet with two voices.
