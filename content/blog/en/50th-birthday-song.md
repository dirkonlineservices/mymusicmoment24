# 50th Birthday Song: Five Decades in One Song

A 50th birthday is a big milestone. Half a century full of stories: school days, first love, an apprenticeship or university, family, career, maybe building a house, many vacations and friendships that have lasted for decades. ## Why a song suits a 50th birthday

At 50, most people already have everything they need. Gadgets and gift vouchers are quickly forgotten. A song of their own, on the other hand:

- **honors a life**, instead of just giving an object,
- **becomes the highlight of the party**, when all the guests listen and laugh along,
- **stays as a memory** and can be played again at their 60th.

## Ideas for the lyrics: five decades, five verses

One idea that works well: each decade gets a small part in the song.

- **The 70s or 80s:** childhood, cassette recorder, the first bike, vacations at Grandma's.
- **The teenage years:** first party, driver's license, first car, favorite band.
- **Their 20s:** apprenticeship, university, the first place of their own, the love of their life.
- **Their 30s and 40s:** family, kids, career, building a house, legendary vacations.
- **Today:** What makes the birthday person who they are? Hobbies, sayings, quirks. And what comes next?

Add a chorus with their name and a message like "50 and still not quiet" or "Here's to the next 50". You don't have to rhyme anything. Bullet points are enough.

## Funny or emotional?

Both work, and often it's the mix that makes it special:

- **Funny:** inside jokes, embarrassing stories (in moderation) and their typical sayings. This fits well with a sing-along Schlager or a rock anthem for the party.
- **Emotional:** thanks to a special person, a look back at the years together. A pop ballad or an acoustic song suits this.
- **Both:** funny verses and a moving chorus. First everyone laughs, and then come the goosebumps.

- **Their partner:** as a declaration of love after many years together.

- **Circle of friends or bowling club:** with all the inside jokes only the group understands.
- **Colleagues:** a song about the team, the office kitchen and the best stories from work.
