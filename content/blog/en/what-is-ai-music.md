## What does AI music actually mean?

AI music (music made with artificial intelligence) refers to melodies, rhythms, vocals and complete arrangements created with trained neural networks.

During training, these systems analyzed a very large number of pieces of music. In the process they learned:

- Which chord progressions sound right in which genre
- How a melody builds emotionally (tension, resolution, chorus)
- How vocals sound, from soft acoustic to powerful pop
- How instruments play together so a song feels "complete"

The result: from a description with your story, your preferred genre and the mood, the system can create a complete piece of music that sounds like a real production.
